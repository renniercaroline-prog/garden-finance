What is Garden Finance?

Garden Finance is a social investment platform that transforms investing from a solitary activity into a community-driven experience. We combine real-time portfolio tracking, leaderboards, and social features to make investing transparent, engaging, and accessible.

Users can track their friends' portfolios, compete on leaderboards, and join investment clubs around shared themes like green energy or women-led startups. The platform uses network analysis and AI to reveal investment patterns, co-holdings, and successful strategies across the community.



Your Personal Investment Garden

Built with an immersive 3D-visualized interface, Garden Finance turns your portfolio into a living, breathing garden:

- 🌱 Each patch represents an investment category - Tech stocks, ETFs, S&P 500, NASDAQ, crypto, and more

- 📈 Watch your garden grow - As your investments perform well, your patches flourish and expand

- 📉 Plants reflect reality - When investments decline, your plants wither

- 🔍 Interactive exploration - Click on any patch to see detailed performance metrics and growth history

- 👥 Visit friends' gardens - Explore your network's portfolios, discover their strategies, and learn from their successes

This visual metaphor makes complex financial data tangible and intuitive—you can literally see your wealth grow and understand the health of your portfolio at a glance.
Why This Matters

Women invest 18% less than men (Female Invest, 2024). Not because they can't, but because traditional finance feels exclusive, intimidating, and lonely. We're changing that by making investing social, transparent, and fun.
